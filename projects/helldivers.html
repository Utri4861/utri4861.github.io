Helldivers 2: B-01 Armor

Video game costume

Complete

My B-01 costume from Helldivers 2 combines printed armor, sewn clothing, aluminum details, nylon webbing, vinyl, and airbrushed paint.

Fit and finish

I adjusted the fit and spacing of the plates alongside the clothing and straps. Sanding and paint finished the printed surfaces.

Game reference

I compared the costume with the game reference while working on the plate shapes, spacing, and paint.
