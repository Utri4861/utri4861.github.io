Plans: Colt Walker Revolver

CAD exploration | Fusion 360

Complete

I modeled the Colt Walker in Fusion 360, then made several variants and an assembly animation. The drawing set documents the parts and assemblies.

Animation

This was my first experiment with Fusion 360’s animation tools. I used an early version of the model to make an exploded assembly sequence.

Variants

The gun-assembly scene in The Good, the Bad, and the Ugly made me want to try swapping parts around the same frame.

  • A frame without the full barrel assembly.
  • An engraved version with a rounded body and tapered barrel.
  • A cartridge-conversion version partly inspired by the Gasser M1870.

I worked from homemade plans found online, then used the base model for these variations.

Drawing set

The 13-sheet drawing set includes the assemblies and individual parts.
